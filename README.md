---

## OBJECTIVE

The objectives of this experiment are:

1. To understand different prompt patterns used in AI-based content generation.
2. To generate an initial draft using simple prompts.
3. To improve the generated content using advanced prompting techniques.
4. To compare different versions of AI-generated content.
5. To evaluate the generated content based on coherence, creativity, accuracy, tone, and structure.

---

# PROCEDURE

### Step 1: Introduction to Prompt Patterns

The following prompt patterns were studied:

* **Query Decomposition:** Dividing a complex task into smaller and manageable sub-tasks.
* **Decision Making:** Asking the AI model to select the most appropriate option from multiple choices.
* **Answer Engineering:** Providing detailed instructions about the expected structure, format, depth, and style of the answer.
* **Fact Check List:** Instructing the model to identify claims that require verification and avoid unsupported information.
* **Tail Generation:** Asking the model to continue and expand an existing piece of content logically.
* **Menu Actions:** Providing several possible actions and allowing the model to select or recommend the most suitable one.
* **Semantic Filter:** Controlling the generated content according to specific requirements such as tone, audience, relevance, complexity, and subject.

### Step 2: Selection of Content Scenarios

Two scenarios were selected for this experiment:

1. **Business Report on Market Trends**
2. **Artificial Intelligence Impact on Healthcare**

### Step 3: Basic Prompt Creation

A simple prompt was initially given to generate basic content for each selected scenario.

### Step 4: Prompt Refinement

The basic prompts were progressively enhanced using query decomposition, decision making, answer engineering, fact checking, and semantic filtering.

### Step 5: Output Generation

The AI model was used to generate:

* First draft
* Refined version
* Multiple versions
* Final polished version

### Step 6: Evaluation

The generated outputs were evaluated based on:

* Coherence
* Creativity
* Accuracy
* Relevance
* Tone and style
* Organization
* Completeness

# TEST CASE 1: BUSINESS REPORT ON MARKET TRENDS

## 1. Topic

**Emerging Market Trends in the Technology Industry**

---

## 2. Basic Prompt

**Prompt:**

> "Write a report about current technology market trends."

### First Draft Output

The technology industry is continuously changing due to advancements in artificial intelligence, cloud computing, cybersecurity, automation, and digital transformation. Businesses are increasingly adopting digital technologies to improve productivity and customer experience.

Artificial intelligence has become an important technology for organizations across different industries. Cloud computing enables businesses to access computing resources without maintaining large physical infrastructure. Cybersecurity is also becoming increasingly important because organizations handle large amounts of digital information.

Other important trends include automation, Internet of Things, data analytics, and software-as-a-service platforms. These technologies are expected to influence businesses and consumers in the coming years.

---

## 3. Refined Prompt Using Query Decomposition

**Prompt:**

> "Create a business report on emerging technology market trends. Break the task into the following sections: introduction, major technology trends, business impact, opportunities, challenges, future outlook, and conclusion. Discuss artificial intelligence, cloud computing, cybersecurity, automation, and IoT. Write the report in a formal style suitable for engineering students."

### Improved Output

The technology market is experiencing rapid transformation as organizations adopt digital technologies to improve operational efficiency and create new business opportunities.

### Major Technology Trends

**Artificial Intelligence:**
AI is being adopted for automation, prediction, customer service, content generation, and decision support.

**Cloud Computing:**
Cloud platforms provide scalable computing, storage, databases, and software services without requiring organizations to maintain all infrastructure themselves.

**Cybersecurity:**
As businesses become increasingly digital, protecting applications, networks, and data has become a major priority.

**Automation:**
Automation helps organizations reduce repetitive manual tasks and improve operational efficiency.

**Internet of Things:**
IoT connects physical devices and sensors to networks, enabling organizations to collect and analyze real-time information.

### Business Impact

These technologies can reduce operational costs, improve productivity, support data-driven decisions, and enable new digital services.

### Opportunities

Organizations can use emerging technologies to develop innovative products, enter new markets, improve customer experiences, and optimize business processes.

### Challenges

Major challenges include cybersecurity threats, implementation costs, shortage of skilled professionals, data privacy concerns, and integration with existing systems.

### Future Outlook

Technology adoption is expected to continue increasing as organizations seek scalable and intelligent digital solutions.

---

## 4. Decision-Making Prompt

**Prompt:**

> "Choose the three most important technology trends for businesses from AI, cloud computing, IoT, cybersecurity, blockchain, and automation. Select them based on business impact, adoption potential, scalability, and long-term relevance. Explain your choices."

### AI Decision Output

The three most important trends are:

1. **Artificial Intelligence** – Because it can automate tasks, analyze data, and support business decisions.
2. **Cloud Computing** – Because it provides scalable and flexible infrastructure.
3. **Cybersecurity** – Because protecting digital infrastructure is essential for every modern organization.

---

## 5. Answer Engineering + Fact Check Prompt

**Prompt:**

> "Rewrite the report as a professional business report with headings and concise paragraphs. Avoid unsupported statistics and exaggerated claims. Clearly distinguish general industry observations from specific factual claims. Include a fact-check section identifying information that should be verified before publication."

### Fact Check List

| Claim/Area                    | Verification Required                  |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| AI adoption                   | Verify using recent industry reports   |
| Cloud computing growth        | Verify using current market research   |
| Cybersecurity risks           | Verify using security reports          |
| IoT adoption                  | Verify using recent industry data      |
| Future technology predictions | Present as forecasts rather than facts |

---

## 6. Semantic Filtering Prompt

**Prompt:**

> "Filter the report for an engineering-student audience. Use formal but simple language. Remove unnecessary marketing language, unsupported statistics, repetition, and overly technical explanations. Keep only information directly related to technology market trends."

### Result

The content became:

* More concise
* More technically relevant
* Easier to understand
* Less repetitive
* More suitable for academic submission

# TEST CASE 2: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IMPACT ON HEALTHCARE

## 1. Topic

**Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Healthcare**

---

## 2. Basic Prompt

**Prompt:**

> "Write an article about the impact of artificial intelligence on healthcare."

### First Draft Output

Artificial intelligence is becoming increasingly important in healthcare. It can assist doctors in analyzing medical information, detecting patterns, supporting diagnosis, and managing healthcare operations.

AI can be used in medical imaging, patient monitoring, drug discovery, virtual assistants, and hospital management. Machine learning algorithms can analyze large amounts of data and identify patterns that may be difficult to detect manually.

However, AI in healthcare also has challenges such as privacy, security, bias, reliability, and the need for human supervision.

---

## 3. Query Decomposition Prompt

**Prompt:**

> "Create an article about the impact of AI on healthcare by completing these tasks separately:
>
> 1. Explain AI in healthcare.
> 2. Identify major applications.
> 3. Explain benefits.
> 4. Explain limitations and risks.
> 5. Discuss the role of healthcare professionals.
> 6. Discuss future possibilities.
> 7. Provide a conclusion.
>    Use formal, educational language."

### Refined Output Structure

**Introduction**

Artificial intelligence refers to computational techniques that can perform tasks such as pattern recognition, prediction, classification, and decision support. Healthcare is one area where these capabilities can assist professionals and organizations.

**Major Applications**

* Medical image analysis
* Clinical decision support
* Patient monitoring
* Drug discovery
* Administrative automation
* Personalized healthcare
* Healthcare chatbots and virtual assistants

**Benefits**

AI can help process large datasets, automate repetitive activities, support clinical workflows, and potentially improve efficiency.

**Challenges**

Important challenges include data privacy, algorithmic bias, model reliability, cybersecurity, explainability, and regulatory requirements.

**Role of Healthcare Professionals**

AI should support rather than automatically replace qualified healthcare professionals. Human expertise remains important for interpreting results, considering patient context, communicating with patients, and making clinical decisions.

**Future Possibilities**

Future healthcare systems may increasingly combine AI with medical devices, electronic health records, robotics, and remote monitoring systems.

**Conclusion**

AI has significant potential to support healthcare, but responsible implementation requires appropriate validation, privacy protection, human oversight, and continuous evaluation.

---

# 4. Menu Actions Prompt

**Prompt:**

> "You are preparing an educational article on AI in healthcare. Choose the most appropriate approach from the following:
> A. Focus only on advantages.
> B. Focus only on technical implementation.
> C. Provide a balanced discussion of applications, benefits, limitations, ethics, and future possibilities.
> D. Write the article as a fictional story.
> Select the best option and explain why."

### Decision

**Selected Option: C**

A balanced discussion is most suitable because healthcare is a high-impact field where both the potential benefits and risks of AI need to be considered.

---

# 5. Tail Generation Prompt

**Prompt:**

> "Continue the article by expanding the section on challenges and ethical considerations. Maintain the same formal tone and connect the new content naturally with the previous section."

### Generated Extension

Another important consideration is the responsible use of patient data. Healthcare AI systems may require access to large datasets, making data protection and secure handling essential. Poor-quality or unrepresentative data can also affect model performance and may contribute to biased outcomes.

Transparency is another challenge. Some AI systems can produce complex predictions that are difficult for users to interpret. Therefore, healthcare organizations need appropriate validation and monitoring processes before relying on AI-assisted outputs.

Human oversight remains essential. AI-generated recommendations should be reviewed within the appropriate professional and clinical context rather than being treated as automatically correct.

---

# 6. Fact Check List

Before publishing the article, the following points should be verified:

| Area                    | Verification                                                |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Medical claims          | Verify using reliable medical sources                       |
| AI capabilities         | Confirm that the stated capability is technically supported |
| Diagnostic applications | Verify evidence and intended use                            |
| Patient privacy         | Check applicable healthcare data regulations                |
| AI limitations          | Compare with current research                               |
| Future predictions      | Clearly label them as possibilities or forecasts            |

---

# 7. Semantic Filtering

**Prompt:**

> "Apply a semantic filter to the article. Keep the content educational, neutral, evidence-oriented, and suitable for engineering students. Remove exaggerated claims, fear-based language, unnecessary jargon, unsupported medical advice, and promotional statements."

### Filtered Result

The final article:

* Uses an academic tone.
* Avoids exaggerated claims.
* Does not present AI as a replacement for doctors.
* Separates current applications from future possibilities.
* Highlights both benefits and risks.
* Uses language suitable for engineering students.

# FINAL VERSION – ARTICLE

## Artificial Intelligence and Its Impact on Healthcare

### Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is becoming an important technology in healthcare. AI systems can process large amounts of information, recognize patterns, automate selected tasks, and provide decision-support capabilities. These applications have the potential to improve healthcare processes when implemented appropriately.

### Applications of AI in Healthcare

AI can be applied in several areas of healthcare. Medical imaging systems can assist professionals in analyzing images and identifying patterns. Machine learning can also be used in research and prediction tasks.

Other applications include patient monitoring, administrative automation, drug discovery, virtual assistants, and personalized healthcare systems.

### Benefits

AI can help healthcare organizations process information efficiently and automate repetitive activities. It may also assist professionals by providing additional information for consideration during appropriate workflows.

### Challenges

The use of AI in healthcare introduces several challenges. Patient information must be handled securely, and systems should be evaluated for reliability and potential bias. The quality and representativeness of training data can influence system performance.

Another challenge is explainability. Users may need to understand how an AI system arrived at a particular output, especially when the output influences important decisions.

### Role of Healthcare Professionals

AI should generally be viewed as a tool that supports qualified healthcare professionals rather than as an automatic replacement for professional judgment. Human oversight is important for interpreting AI outputs and considering patient-specific circumstances.

### Future Scope

AI may become increasingly integrated with electronic health records, medical devices, remote monitoring systems, robotics, and other digital healthcare technologies. Future developments will require careful testing, responsible data management, appropriate regulation, and continuous monitoring.

### Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence has significant potential to support healthcare through medical analysis, automation, monitoring, research, and decision support. However, successful adoption requires attention to privacy, security, bias, reliability, explainability, and human oversight. Therefore, AI should be implemented as a responsible support technology alongside qualified healthcare professionals.

# OBSERVATION

The experiment demonstrated that the quality of AI-generated content depends significantly on the structure of the prompt. A basic prompt produced a general response, whereas structured prompts generated more organized, focused, and detailed content.

The use of query decomposition improved the logical structure. Decision-making prompts helped select an appropriate direction. Answer engineering improved formatting and audience suitability. Fact-check prompts increased attention to accuracy, while semantic filtering removed irrelevant and exaggerated content. Tail generation helped expand existing content without losing continuity.
